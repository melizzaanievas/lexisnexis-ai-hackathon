# Experiment Log: Legal AI Disclaimers, Creator Credits & Editable Slide Export

> **Project:** Protégé Scenario-to-Authority Educational Engine  
> **Experiment Focus:** Terms & Conditions, Practitioner Verification Rules & Multi-Format Slide Generation  
> **Date:** August 2026  
> **Author:** Melizza Anievas (LexisNexis AI Hackathon 2026)  

---

## 🔬 1. Objectives & Compliance Framework

1. **Practitioner & AI Verification Safeguards:** Implement explicit Terms & Conditions requiring legal practitioners and students to independently verify all statutory and judicial outputs generated by AI models.
2. **Product & Creator Attribution:** Provide direct navigation links to LexisNexis Protégé and credit the developer (Melizza Anievas) with GitHub and LinkedIn profile anchors.
3. **Editable Presentation Generation:** Integrate client-side `.pptx` slide generation (PptxGenJS) enabling one-click exports that users can open or upload directly into Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Key Dashboard Additions (`index.html`)

### **A. Legal AI Usage Disclaimer & Terms of Conditions**
Added a visible legal disclaimer box directly above the footer:
> *"⚠️ Terms & Conditions & AI Legal Disclaimer: This dashboard is an educational research tool powered by LexisNexis Protégé and generative AI models. Outputs do not constitute formal legal advice. Lawyers, legal practitioners, and users must independently verify all statutory provisions, case law citations, and legal analyses prior to professional or judicial reliance."*

### **B. One-Click Editable Presentation Engine (`.pptx` / Google Slides)**
Integrated the `PptxGenJS` library to generate 16:9 widescreen presentations client-side:
* **Slide 1:** Scenario Title & Factual Overview
* **Slide 2:** Dual-Verdict Legal Analysis (Gross Misconduct vs. Minor Negligence under Cap. 57 s.9)
* **Slide 3:** Non-Lawyer Stakeholder & Client Takeaways
* **Slide 4:** University Legal Clinic Exercises (True/False)

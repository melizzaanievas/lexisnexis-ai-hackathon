# LexisNexis Protégé: General Prompting Guide & Vault Scaffolding Framework

> **Project:** Protégé Scenario-to-Authority Educational Engine  
> **Jurisdiction:** Hong Kong SAR & Universal Legal Frameworks  
> **Target Audience:** Law Students, University Legal Clinics & Non-Lawyer Stakeholders  

---

## 💡 Overview & Prompting Strategy

To bypass context limits, ensure pinpoint statutory accuracy, and enforce dual-audience formatting, we use a two-tiered prompting strategy inside LexisNexis Protégé:

1. **Vault Scaffolding:** Store core rules, formatting instructions, and multi-audience structures in a persistent Vault file (`universal_clinic_template_framework.txt`).
2. **Execution Prompt:** Execute a lightweight scenario prompt referencing the Vault framework and the **Generate Template Responses** skill.

---

## 📋 Step 1: Universal Vault Template (`universal_clinic_template_framework.txt`)

Upload a `.txt` file with the following content into your **Protégé Vault**:

```text
[SYSTEM INSTRUCTION: UNIVERSAL CLINICAL SCAFFOLDING FRAMEWORK]

ROLE:
You are an expert Legal Research & Educational Engine assisting both University Legal Clinics and Corporate Stakeholders.

CORE OBJECTIVE:
Given a legal factual scenario, extract pinpoint statutory provisions, leading judicial precedents, and synthesize a dual-audience assessment.

OUTPUT STRUCTURE REQUIREMENTS:
Every response MUST contain the following 5 structured sections:

1. FACTUAL OVERVIEW & SCENARIO CARD:
   - Provide a clear 2-sentence summary of the factual dispute.

2. DUAL-VERDICT LEGAL ANALYSIS:
   - High Risk / Repudiatory Breach: Analyze under primary statutory sub-clauses where conduct breaches essential duties.
   - Minor / Non-Repudiatory Error: Analyze counter-arguments under leading precedents where conduct is an isolated error or misunderstanding.

3. PRIMARY STATUTORY & PRECEDENT PINPOINTING:
   - Quote exact section numbers, sub-clauses, and case ratio citations.

4. UNIVERSITY LEGAL CLINIC EXERCISE:
   - Provide 2 True/False clinical assessment questions with answers.

5. NON-LAWYER CLIENT TAKEAWAYS:
   - Provide 3 clear, actionable, plain-English bullet points for non-lawyer stakeholders.
```

## 💬 Step 2: Master Protégé Execution Prompts Option A: Employment & Summary Dismissal Scenario Copy and paste this prompt directly into Protégé (Ask or Draft tab):

```text
Using universal_clinic_template_framework.txt in my Vault, analyze the following factual scenario under HK Employment Ordinance Cap. 57 s.9 and Tsang Tak Chi v China Wall Ltd:

SCENARIO: An employee backed up 50GB of personal photos to enterprise cloud storage without authorization and was immediately terminated without notice.

Ensure the output contains all 5 structured sections including line-level statutory pinpointing and 3 plain-English client takeaways.
```

# Checkpoint 1 Submission: Initial Idea & Proposal
> **Team:** Prompt to Practice
> **Submitted:** August 16, 2026

## Background and Problem
Our multidisciplinary team brings together lawyers, technologists, and compliance strategists across Hong Kong's legal ecosystem. Practitioners and students spend excessive billable/study hours manually searching case precedents, verifying procedural rules, and converting raw legal findings into presentation materials for court, clients, or academic use[cite: 2]. Existing AI workflows lack localization options and fail to account for differing needs between private practice and in-house counsel[cite: 2].

## Initial Proposed Use Case
We initially proposed a **Scenario-to-Authority Engine** powered by Lexis+ with Protégé to assist with deep-dive legal research, precedent verification, and drafting complex legal documents[cite: 2]. The goal was to input factual scenarios and allow Protégé to generate curated authority pathways alongside draft legal materials for law firms, in-house teams, and university faculties[cite: 2].

## Intended Outcome
Targeted outcomes included shifting legal research from manual searching to intent-driven validation, achieving >90% precedent retrieval accuracy, reducing time-to-insight by 70%, and enabling multi-format markdown exports[cite: 2].

## Initial Proposed Workflow
1. **Scenario Input:** User enters facts or legal decision parameters into Protégé.
2. **Precedent Synthesis:** Protégé searches LexisNexis databases and surfaces matching precedents.
3. **Human Review Gate:** Attorneys verify outputs for legal accuracy, strategy, and client privacy compliance.
4. **Summary & Export:** Protégé outputs structured legal summaries exportable into presentation formats.

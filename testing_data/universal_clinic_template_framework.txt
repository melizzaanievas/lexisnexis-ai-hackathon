DOCUMENT TITLE: Universal Legal Clinic Educational Scaffolding Framework
PURPOSE: Reusable Educational Template for Plain-English Legal Scenario Analysis
DATABASE CONTEXT: Works across all primary statutes, case laws, and regulatory frameworks in the LexisNexis database.

WORKFLOW & METHODOLOGY:
When requested to generate a legal clinic display board or educational material:
1. Grounding & Search: Query the primary statutes, regulations, and case precedents specified in the user inputs.
2. Dual-Threshold Analysis: Analyze the scenario facts against the two competing legal interpretations (e.g., High-Threshold Liability/Misconduct vs. Low-Threshold Negligence/Permissible Exception).
3. Plain-English Rendering: Translate complex legal holdings into accessible, plain-English educational display cards.

REQUIRED OUTPUT TEMPLATE:

1. **The Scenario Card:**
   A 3-sentence plain-English story describing [PARTY_NAME], a [ROLE] in the [INDUSTRY] sector, who [INCIDENT_ACTION_OR_BREACH] and faced [LEGAL_CONSEQUENCE_OR_DISMISSAL]. State that [WORKER_OR_PARTY_NAME] seeks clarification under [PRIMARY_STATUTE_OR_LAW].

2. **The Verdict:**
   - **[HIGH_THRESHOLD_VIOLATION, e.g., Gross Misconduct / Material Breach]:** [2-3 sentences explaining why the conduct could satisfy the higher legal threshold under [PRIMARY_STATUTE_OR_LAW] based on duty of fidelity, policy breach, statutory violation, or industry-specific risk].
   - **[LOW_THRESHOLD_DEFENSE, e.g., Minor Negligence / Mitigating Factor]:** [2-3 sentences explaining why, under [PRIMARY_PRECEDENT], the legal consequence may be disproportionate, and why this act could instead be classified as an error of judgment, minor oversight, or excusable breach].

3. **Clinical Exercise:**
   - Question 1 (True/False): [A single T/F question testing the statutory threshold or primary rule under [PRIMARY_STATUTE_OR_LAW]].
   - Question 2 (True/False): [A single T/F question testing the judicial distinction or exception established in [PRIMARY_PRECEDENT]].
